### Q3. [Frontend] Polling vs WebSockets
When designing a real-time notification mechanism, why is standard HTTP Polling often considered more expensive in terms of server resource overhead compared to WebSockets?

**答案**: 因为HTTP Polling 必须每几秒就要建立和销毁HTTP请求。而 WebSockets 一旦建立通道，就是一个长期连接管道。

> 💡 **解析**：**[x] 回答正确**。Polling 频繁带来 TCP/TLS 握手以及 Header 重复传输，白白浪费大量服务器算力与带宽。它已晋升至 Box 2。
